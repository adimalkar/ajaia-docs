# Product brief: Team Docs

A **lightweight** editor for shared work. Imported from *Markdown*.

## Goals

- Create and format documents quickly
- Share with teammates as **viewer** or **editor**
- Keep everything saved automatically

## Next steps

1. Pilot with two teams
2. Collect feedback on sharing
3. Decide on comments vs. version history

> Markdown headings, emphasis, lists and quotes all carry over.
